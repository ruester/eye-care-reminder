    For everyone who works at a screen

    Give your eyes a break.

    Eye Care Reminder is a lightweight desktop app that reminds you to rest your eyes at an interval you choose, so long screen sessions don't end with tired, dry or strained eyes.

    Free and open source · Windows 10 (1903 or later), Windows 11 and Linux · Version 1.2.0

    Time for an eye break Look at something at least 6 metres (20 feet) away for 20 seconds and blink a few times.
    ✕

    Small app, healthy habit

    It sits quietly in the notification area and only speaks up when it's time for a break.

    Your rhythm

    Choose any interval from 1 minute to 24 hours. By default you're reminded once an hour.

    Reminders you'll actually see

    A clear popup in the bottom-right corner that doesn't steal focus from what you're working on, even with Do Not Disturb on.

    Hides by itself

    A countdown bar shows when the reminder disappears. Pick 1 second to 5 minutes, or keep it until you click it.

    Break when it suits you

    "Take a break now" starts a break right away and the next reminder is timed from then. Locking your PC counts as a break, too.

    Speaks your language

    Available in 13 languages and set to your system language automatically. You can switch at any time in the settings.

    Light and private

    Starts automatically when you log in and needs no account and no internet connection. No data is collected.

    How it works

    1. 1

      Install

      Run the installer. Eye Care Reminder starts right away and from then on whenever you log in.

    2. 2

      Set your interval

      Click the eye icon in the notification area to choose how often you want to be reminded.

    3. 3

      Look away

      When the reminder appears, look at something far away for 20 seconds and blink a few times.

    Frequently asked questions

    Windows shows "Windows protected your PC". Is that safe?

    The installer isn't signed with a paid code-signing certificate, so Microsoft Defender SmartScreen warns about it when it's new. Click More info and then Run anyway to install. The installer is built from the public source code on GitHub.

    How do I change the interval, the auto-hide time or the language?

    Click the eye icon in the notification area (on Windows 11 it may be behind the ^ arrow) or open Eye Care Reminder from the Start menu. "Test notification" shows you a reminder with your settings.

    Does it still remind me when Do Not Disturb is on?

    Yes. Eye Care Reminder draws its own reminder instead of using Windows notifications, which Windows hides while Do Not Disturb or Focus is on.

    How do I update or uninstall it?

    To update, run the newer installer: it replaces the installed version and keeps your settings. To uninstall on Windows, go to Settings → Apps → Installed apps and remove Eye Care Reminder. On Linux, run sh ~/.local/share/eye-care-reminder/uninstall.sh.

    Which Linux desktops are supported?

    Xfce, Ubuntu Unity, GNOME, KDE Plasma and the i3 window manager are supported; the installer sets up what each needs for the tray icon (e.g. the AppIndicator extension on GNOME). The app also runs on other desktops such as Cinnamon or MATE. The reminder always gets through: if the app's own popup can't be shown, it falls back to the desktop's notifications, notify-send or a simple window. You can always open the settings from the application menu.
